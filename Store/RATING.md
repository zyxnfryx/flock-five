# App Store rating

List Flock Five as **9+** (or 12+), not Kids.

The garden, bee silhouettes, and snore Zzz will look young. Kids category plus ads is a 1.3 / COPPA dead end.

When ads go live, flip NSPrivacyTracking in Assets/Plugins/iOS/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy and add the advertising data types the network actually collects.
