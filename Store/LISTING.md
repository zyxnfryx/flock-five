## Description

Flock Five is a portrait puzzle about tropical hummingbirds on garden branches.

Tap a branch, tap a destination. Same-color birds hop together. Get five of a live color on a matching feeder and they flock. The feeder whooshes out. The branch snaps.

Some birds hide under bees. The bees lift only when that bird is actually at the tip. Later gardens, bees sit on the tip itself — flock another color and the breeze lifts them. Some evenings only one feeder hangs. Fill a color with no matching feeder and they nap until one hangs. Hang it at the right time and you chain a combo.

Fifteen gardens:

- Dawn Garden
- Bee Thicket
- Noon Queue
- Dusk Scatter
- Moonrise Nap
- Night Lattice
- Dew Arcade
- Pollen Court
- Hive Porch
- Amber Grove
- Sun Hive
- Thistle Well
- Nectar Pinch
- Twin Swarm
- Last Light

Clear a garden and the next one loads. Restart stays on the garden you are on.

No account. No chat. Just the garden.
